Stage 1: Lead-In (5-8 Mins)

Icebreaker: Travel Mishap Roulette

Click the button to draw a discussion card with your partner or practice answering aloud.

Travel Experience Question 1 of 5

"Have you ever missed a bus, train, or flight? What happened, and how did you resolve the situation?"

Speaking Tips for B1 Learners:

• Use time linkers: "A few years ago...", "While I was waiting...", "Suddenly I realized...", "Eventually..."

Aim for at least 30-45 seconds of continuous speaking.

Example Model Answer (B1 Target):

"Yes, definitely! Two summers ago, I was travelling to Barcelona by train. While I was waiting at the station, I was reading a book and didn't notice the departure platform had changed. When I finally arrived at the gate, the doors were already locked! At first, I felt panic, but I immediately went to the customer desk. Fortunately, the staff were sympathetic and transferred my ticket to the next departure without any extra fee."

High-Frequency B1 Travel Collocations

Verb + Noun

Miss a connecting flight

To arrive too late for the second leg of your airplane journey.

"Because our first plane was delayed by 40 minutes, we missed our connecting flight in Frankfurt."
Noun Phrase

Lost luggage / baggage

Suitcases that fail to appear on the carousel at baggage reclaim.

"We stood waiting at baggage claim for an hour before filing a report for our lost luggage."
Verb + Noun

File a formal complaint

To officially submit a written problem to a company or customer service.

"The hotel room was completely dirty, so I decided to file a formal complaint with the manager."
Collocation

Fully booked / overbooked

Having no vacant rooms, seats, or tickets left available.

"Unfortunately, all budget hotels in the city centre were fully booked for the festival weekend."
Noun

Compensation & Refund

Money or vouchers paid back to a traveler for inconvenience or cancelled services.

"Because the flight was cancelled without warning, passengers were entitled to €250 in compensation."
Idiom / Phrasal

Stranded (somewhere)

Unable to leave a place because of transport problems or bad weather.

"When the last bus broke down on the mountain road, we were stranded until midnight."

Stage 4: Grammar for Fluency (10 Mins)

Narrative Tenses: Setting the Scene vs Main Action

B1 speakers must combine the Past Continuous (background) and Past Simple (interrupting event).

Grammar Formula at B1:
Background Action (Past Continuous)

was / were + verb-ing

"While we were waiting at the boarding gate..."

Describes the ongoing longer activity in progress.

Interrupting Event (Past Simple)

regular -ed / irregular form

"...suddenly the airline staff announced a 4-hour delay."

Short completed action that interrupts the background.

Notice the connector words: while / as (+ continuous) vs when / suddenly (+ simple).

Interactive Quick Check: Choose the natural B1 sentence:

1. "While I ____________ along the canal in Venice, my passport ____________ out of my bag!"

2. "When we finally arrived at the hotel, the receptionist said they ____________ our reservation because of a computer glitch."

Role A: Dissatisfied Guest

The Tired Traveler

You booked a sea-view quiet room 2 months ago. Upon arrival, the receptionist tells you the hotel is overbooked and offers you a tiny basement room next to the kitchen with no window.

Your Secret Objectives:

• Politely refuse the basement room.

• Demand free breakfast or an upgrade at a partner hotel.

• Maintain a calm, professional tone using: "I'm afraid that's unacceptable..."

Role B: Reception Manager

The Hotel Duty Manager

Due to a burst water pipe on the 4th floor, 10 rooms are uninhabitable. You want to keep the guest calm and avoid negative TripAdvisor reviews, but your hotel is genuinely completely full.

Your Secret Objectives:

• Apologize sincerely and explain the water pipe emergency.

• Offer free buffet breakfast and 30% discount voucher.

• If they insist, call the 4-star boutique hotel 2 blocks away and pay for a taxi.
